## 0.4.0 — 2026-08-05

First public release.

- Displays the selected waveform region of the active QLab 5 Audio cue.
- Uses hybrid OSC and AppleScript monitoring with smooth local progress interpolation.
- Shows elapsed and remaining time, playback state, monitoring diagnostics, and Auto-follow state.
- Adds countdown alerts at 30, 15, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 seconds remaining.
- Keeps countdown digits white and flashes a fading orange gradient behind the final 15% of the waveform.
- Supports Intel and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS Big Sur 11 or newer.
